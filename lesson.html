Lesson Overview

Every learning journey begins with understanding the basics. This lesson introduces the topic and helps you build a strong foundation.

Read each section carefully. Take your time, think about the examples and try the practice activity at the end.

💡 Learning tip: Understanding a concept is more important than memorising it. Try to explain what you learn using your own words.
