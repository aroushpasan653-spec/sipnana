Cultural Triangle

Sigiriya Lion Rock

Ancient rock fortress with frescoes, mirror wall, and panoramic views. Ideal to visit early in the morning to avoid crowds and heat.

  • Best Time: 6:30 AM – 9:30 AM (Jan – Apr)
  • How to Reach: Bus/Tuk-tuk from Dambulla (~30 mins)
  • Self-Travel Tip: Carry water and start climbing before sun gets strong.
